# Contractions

A simple contraction timer with your maternity contact close by.

## Time a contraction

1. Tap **Start contraction** when it begins.
2. Tap **Stop contraction** when it ends.
3. Repeat for the next contraction. Each timing is saved automatically; details can be added later.

A running timer survives a reload. If timing was interrupted, check or adjust the record before relying on it.

## What it records

- **Duration:** how long each contraction lasts.
- **Start-to-start spacing:** beginning of one contraction to the beginning of the next.
- **Rest:** end of a contraction to the start of the next.
- Optional perceived intensity, pain location, comfort measures and notes.
- Separate events such as waters breaking, a show, comfort measures, or contacting your maternity team.

**Timeline** lets you review, search, edit and delete records. **Overview** shows recent averages, a duration chart and a printable summary. **Care & data** keeps your maternity contact and instructions, backups, CSV exports and home-screen installation steps.

## Your data

Records stay in your browser on this device. They are separate from the headache diary and do not sync across devices. Download backups regularly, especially before changing browsers or clearing browser data. Imports merge records, keep newer edits, and fill empty care settings without replacing existing values.

## Getting support

Follow your maternity team’s advice and contact them whenever you are concerned. This app records observations; it cannot diagnose labour, estimate dilation or decide when it is safe to stay home. Do not wait for a timer pattern before seeking help for concerning symptoms.
